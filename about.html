About Oceanhold

Connecting global buyers with India’s agricultural diversity through thoughtful sourcing, product knowledge and dependable export coordination.

Our Story

A contemporary Indian export house

India has a remarkable agricultural diversity, shaped by its regions, farmers and traditional knowledge. At Oceanhold Exports, we work with a network of suppliers across the country to connect this diversity with international markets.

Based in Pattambi, Kerala, we began with a simple belief: every shipment represents more than just products — it represents trust, quality and the rich agricultural heritage of India.

Our focus is on understanding buyer requirements, identifying suitable sourcing options and coordinating the details needed to move products into international trade. We proudly partner with importers, distributors, wholesalers and food manufacturers across global markets.

Our Mission
