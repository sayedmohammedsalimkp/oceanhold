About Oceanhold

An India-based sourcing & export partner

India has a remarkable agricultural diversity, shaped by its regions, farmers and traditional knowledge. Oceanhold Exports works with a network of suppliers across the country to connect this diversity with international markets.

We focus on understanding what each buyer needs — product, grade, quantity, packaging and destination — then identify suitable sourcing options and coordinate the details that move products into international trade.

“Every shipment represents more than just products — it represents trust, quality and the agricultural heritage of India.”

Export Capabilities

Quality, packaging & paperwork — coordinated

Every shipment is prepared to protect product quality through storage and international transport, and backed by the documentation your market requires.

  • Registered & licensed — FSSAI, APEDA and Spices Board of India.
  • Shipment documents — Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary, Certificate of Analysis and Fumigation (when required).
  • Packaging options — bulk bags, woven PP, kraft pouches, export cartons and private label with multi-language labels.
