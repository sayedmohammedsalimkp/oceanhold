Our Sourcing

Sourcing through the right processing partners.

We source agricultural products from selected processing units in India, where produce is cleaned, graded and prepared for market. Our role is to connect these products with international buyers and coordinate their specific requirements.

How We Work

Closer to the people who prepare your products.

Behind every agricultural product is a processing unit responsible for preparing it for the market. We work with selected units to source products based on buyer requirements, with attention to grading, specifications, availability and packaging.

  • Sourcing through selected processing units.
  • Each product checked for supplier reliability, specification, quality, availability and export readiness.
  • Clear communication on what we handle directly and what we coordinate through service partners.
The Process

Five steps from requirement to export

01

Understand Requirements

Product, grade, specifications, quantity, packaging and destination.

02

Identify Processing Units

Identify suitable units that can meet the required product specifications and volume.

03

Review Product & Specifications

Review product details, samples, grades and quality requirements. Arrange laboratory testing where required.

04

Confirm Commercial Terms

Finalise pricing, packaging, Incoterms, payment terms and timelines.

05

Coordinate Export

Arrange applicable documentation, logistics and shipment coordination.

Regulatory Compliance

The right registrations for every product.

Export requirements vary across product categories and destination markets. We identify the applicable registrations, certificates and regulatory requirements for each product and coordinate the necessary compliance before shipment.

Exporter Registration

RCMC and other applicable exporter registrations based on the product category.

Product-Specific Compliance

Relevant registrations and approvals identified according to the product being exported.

Shipment Certifications

Certificates and supporting documents arranged as required by the product and destination market.

Shipment Documentation

Documentation for every export requirement.

Export documentation varies by product and destination. We coordinate the applicable certificates and shipping documents to support a smooth export process.

Certificate of Origin

Identifies the country where the product originates.

Phytosanitary Certificate

Issued where required for applicable plant products.

Certificate of Analysis

Provides laboratory test results against specified parameters.

Fumigation Certificate

Provided when required by the product or importing country’s regulations.
