Our Sourcing

A supplier network, coordinated by one accountable partner

We don’t own farms or factories — we connect you with the right ones. Here is how we turn a buyer requirement into a quality-checked, documented export shipment.

How We Work

Sourcing agent and exporter, in one

Oceanhold works with a network of growers, processors and suppliers across India. We act on your behalf — finding the right source, checking the product and coordinating everything needed to get it to your port.

  • One point of contact across multiple products and suppliers.
  • Each product checked for supplier reliability, specification, quality, availability and export readiness.
  • Clear communication on what we handle directly and what we coordinate through service partners.
The Process

Five steps from requirement to export

01

Understand the requirement

Product, intended application, grade, quantity, destination, packaging and target delivery window.

02

Identify suitable suppliers

Explore our Indian supplier network for options that match your specification and volume.

03

Review product & quality

Check specifications, samples, supplier documents and independent laboratory testing where arranged.

04

Coordinate commercial terms

Confirm price basis, quantity, packaging, Incoterms, payment terms and lead time.

05

Coordinate export

Cleaning, packing, documentation, freight and shipment updates — according to the agreed scope.

Shipment Documentation

Complete, accurate & internationally accepted

Every shipment is backed by documentation for a smooth and secure trade. Requirements differ by product and destination — we confirm them with you up front.

Certificate of Origin

Confirms the product’s country of origin.

Phytosanitary Certificate

Certifies the shipment is pest and disease free.

Certificate of Analysis

Laboratory test report verifying product quality.

Fumigation Certificate

Provided when required by the importing country.
